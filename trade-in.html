Once You Accept

Selling your car does not have to be a hassle. From the moment you take the offer, the four things below are ours to do.

  1. 01.

    Accept the Offer

    Take the number as it stands, or send us photographs and the original window sticker and we will sharpen it. The more we can see, the closer the offer gets.

  2. 02.

    Pick Up or Deliver

    We arrange transport to Heath, or you drop the car off yourself at a time that suits you. Either way the move is booked before anything else happens.

  3. 03.

    Inspection on Arrival

    The car is inspected when it reaches us, against the description it arrived with. Nothing is renegotiated on the doorstep for something you already told us.

  4. 04.

    Paperwork and Payment

    Title transfer is done in house, and any outstanding finance or lease payoff is handled with it. There are no hidden fees taken out of the number you accepted.

We Have Answers

What are the steps to sell my vehicle?
  1. Fill out the form with all the required details, upload pictures, and an original window sticker if available. The more information you provide, the more accurate a value we can give.
  2. Accept the offer and we will arrange shipping to Hinderer Motor Company.
  3. On arrival the vehicle is inspected, and we take care of the rest: all the DMV paperwork, with the title transfer done in house, and any outstanding payoff or lease payments and their paperwork. There are no hidden fees.
What information do I need to provide to get the most accurate offer?

Year, make, model and mileage start it. Photographs, the service history and the original window sticker are what move a number from an estimate to an offer — anything that shows condition and specification is worth sending.

How long does it take to get paid?

Payment follows the inspection on arrival, once the title transfer and any outstanding finance payoff are settled. Call 740.618.2466 and we will tell you exactly where your own sale stands.

What if my car is leased or financed?

That is ordinary and it is ours to handle. We settle any outstanding finance or lease payoff and the paperwork that comes with it, in house, as part of the sale.

What vehicle makes does Hinderer Motor Company buy?

All of them. Backdraft, Shelby and Superformance roadsters are what we know best, but the offer form takes any make and model — that is what "we buy all makes and models" means.
