Build your Own!

Craft Your Ultimate Driving Machine

Build your Backdraft RT4B the way you want it, with precision engineering and customizable luxury at every step!

Tailor Your Interior

Make the inside of your roadster as stunning as the exterior. Choose from a range of Interior Upholstery options, including premium materials like red, grey, or tan leather. Elevate your style further with custom Interior Stitching options such as diamond stitch or ribbed patterns, adding both comfort and flair to your ride.

Choose Your Body & Suspension

Make a statement with a custom Body that reflects your taste. Choose from an array of paint finishes, racing stripes, and more. Pair it with a high-performance Suspension system to ensure your RT4B handles the road like a dream, whether you’re cruising or tearing up the track.

Personalize Your Dash & Gauges

Your control center should be as sophisticated as your roadster. Select from sleek, modern Dash & Gauges options, including carbon fiber finishes paired with premium Moal or GT gauges, providing that perfect blend of vintage appeal and contemporary performance.

Let’s Get Started

Upgrade your vehicle quickly and easily. Trade in your car for cash or a new ride—hassle-free. Get started with these simple steps:

  1. 01.

    Get a Quote

    Provide details about your vehicle, including its make, model, mileage, and condition, and receive a fair market value estimate.

  2. 02.

    Schedule an Inspection

    Bring your car in or schedule a virtual inspection so we can assess its condition and finalize the offer.

  3. 03.

    We’ll provide a competitive offer based on the inspection, which you can accept on the spot.

  4. 04.

    Complete the Trade

    Once you accept the offer, you can either use the value toward a new vehicle or receive cash for your trade-in!
